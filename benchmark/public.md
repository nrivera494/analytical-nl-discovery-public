## Deformed variants

Use these to test discovery when recall of the classic answer is likely.

- Nonlinearity $|\psi|^{2\sigma}\psi$ with gain: profile and mode spectrum as functions of $\sigma$.
- Pure-quartic or mixed second- and fourth-order dispersion.
- Gain $g\propto z^{-p}$, linear-slope spectral gain, saturable gain with finite recovery length.
- Index profile exponent $\alpha\ne2$: self-imaging and parametric-instability frequencies.
- Modified Raman response (single Lorentzian with altered width): self-frequency-shift coefficient.
- Synthetic propagation-constant spacing $\beta_j\propto j^\kappa$: thermalization scaling.
