## Goal

De Roeck, Huveneers and Prosniak (CMP 406, 155 (2025), arXiv:2308.11243) state that their localization technique fails for the nonlinear Schrodinger equation because DNLS small denominators E_nu+E_mu-E_kappa-E_lambda involve correlated Anderson eigenvalues, controlled only for pairs. By exact diagonalization of the 1D Anderson model at two disorder strengths, measure the probability that such a quadruple combination within one localization volume is below delta, as a function of delta and volume, and test whether a Minami-type bound (linear in delta, polynomial in volume) is plausible. This decides whether lifting the 2026 sub-power peak bound (arXiv:2502.02344) to linear hopping is blocked by eigenvalue correlations. Exact diagonalization only; record runs.
