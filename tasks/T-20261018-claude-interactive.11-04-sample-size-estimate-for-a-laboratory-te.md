## Goal

The manuscript's outlook says per-realization modal decompositions must resolve the pooled excited-mode g2 to better than 2/M, but gives no number. Compute, from the Dirichlet law itself, the number of independent realizations needed to separate 2 - 2/M from 2 and to resolve the sign change of Cov(I0, I_q) at D/S1, for M = 15-45 and E'/(kappa N) in the condensed range, including the correlation between modes; compare with the 2 x 300 decompositions of Baudin et al., PRL 130, 063801 (2023).
