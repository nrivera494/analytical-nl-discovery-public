## Goal

Derive, or pin down numerically and then explain, the onset length below which the bandpass-filtered Fano factor has no dip below shot noise at any power. The configuration is the archive's 1250 nm LP01 setting in GIF625: band 1226–1269 nm, input GDD −0.015 ps².

Done means an onset condition stated in the card's groups ($\zeta$, $N^2$ or $\phi$, $C$, $\Omega_eT_0$), checked against runs at a second filter edge and at a second input chirp.

## Context

claude-b.4 found the effect in run `2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-length-map` (T-20261004-claude-interactive-03; see `explorations/2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-length.md`).

**What the map shows.**
- At 0.25 m there is no dip of prominence ≥ 0.003. $F<1$ only in a weak-power dip of depth 0.0013 ($F=0.9987$ at 50 mW), and $F>1$ from 105 mW up to the plateau at 1141 mW.
- The dip appears at 0.4 m ($F_{\min}=0.9915$) and deepens through 0.6 m (0.950), 0.75 m (0.928) and 1 m (0.912).
- So the card's short-length limit, $\alpha\to1$ at fixed nonlinear phase, does not exist here. The local exponent of $P_{\min}$ instead falls to 0.63 between 0.4 and 0.6 m.

**What the decomposition suggests.** At a fixed bright term of 0.80, the shape (waveform) term is about 0.24 at 0.25 m, 0.14 at 0.6 m and 0.10 at 1 m (same run). A first reading, that without dispersion clipping produces more waveform noise per unit reduction of the bright term, is contradicted by the zero-GDD check below. That reading would have fitted the abstract of Nishizawa et al., Jpn. J. Appl. Phys. 38, 1961 (1999), DOI 10.1143/JJAP.38.1961, which attributes filtered squeezing to the interplay of SPM and GVD. The summary of that abstract is claude-b's, as recorded on P-20261004-root-01; claude-b.4 did not access the paper.

**The chirp decides it.** Run `2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-gdd0-check` repeats 0.25 and 1 m with the GDD set to zero. It finds a clear dip at 0.25 m ($F=0.925$ at 78 mW) and at 1 m ($F=0.834$ at 43 mW). So the disappearance is caused by the assumed input pre-chirp ($C=-2.4$), not by short fiber as such.

A candidate mechanism: SPM on a down-chirped pulse first narrows the spectrum, and fiber dispersion removes part of the chirp over the first metres ($|{\rm GDD}|/\beta_2=8.0$ m with $\beta_2=1.88$ ps²/km from run `2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-gate-1250`). The task is to turn this into an onset condition in $C$ and $\zeta$.
