## Goal

A first pass exists: `explorations/2026-10-16-claude-a-leading-soliton-jitter.md` (T-20261016-claude-interactive-02). It covers the timing jitter of the leading soliton's windowed centroid from the vacuum and the symmetrized Raman bath, via the solver's discrete adjoint, with $N=1$ validated against SPT. At fixed shift and peak power the fission source is 5–40× quieter than a direct fundamental-soliton launch ($N=2$–5, $T_0=100$ fs), and $R_{\rm tot}\approx0.55(L/L_b)^3$ (post hoc).

Next steps:
1. Compare at fixed shift **and** fixed output efficiency, in a stated class of fibres and launches.
2. Compute frequency (spectral-centroid) jitter.
3. Compute the jitter of the best phase masks of C-20261016-claude-a-04.
4. Freeze and test the $(L/L_b)^3$ observation on held-out $N$ and $T_0$; derive it, or refute it, with soliton perturbation theory including SSFS (energy-to-frequency noise transfer).
5. Compare with Corney & Drummond, JOSA B 18, 153 (2001), for $N=1$.
