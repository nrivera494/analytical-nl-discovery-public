## Goal

The archive's two other preserved 1550 nm LP01 readout recipes exist as registered runs, with their archive values reproduced or every difference quantified, and a convergence check.

- **30 m prepared sech.** Same prepared input as run `2026-10-04-claude-b.1-prepared-sech-readout-20m`, 30 m, η = 0.995, r = 0.482, ψ = +0.203 rad. Archive F = 0.02360533876808313 (oscillator Raman) and 0.04203394529523197 (anisotropic), readout power 37.00391455367498 / 36.903678274574716 mW, on the tight grid. These are the deepest squeezing values in the archive, and the point sits at ζ ≈ 16.4 rather than 10.9.
- **Simple 24 nm recipe.** Flat 1538–1562 nm input band, GDD +0.015 ps², 47.892 mW, 20 m, r = 0.51, ψ = −0.66 rad. Archive F = 0.14674329652959062 / 0.17305429567657293 on its 80 ps check grid.
