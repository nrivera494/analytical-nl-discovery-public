## Goal

The second-family review (2026-10-20) found that in the card's picosecond window (W_t = t_c^2/(|beta2| L_NL) >> 1, R_d << 1) the fast Kerr+disorder operator conserves photon number and transverse energy per time slice, so the local equilibrium is a spatial RJ state with multipliers theta_0(t), theta_2(t), not the joint three-multiplier state. Derive the Raman source terms on that manifold. Raman couples neighbouring time slices through T_R d/dt of the modal beats; its first-order modal effect vanishes for time-symmetric launches (C-20261020-claude-interactive.21-01). Include the noise-seeded Stokes channel (L_th ~ 31 L_NL) and pump-Stokes walk-off (0.74 m per ps), and state when L_kin << min(L_th, L_R^mod) holds.
