## Goal

Replace the independent-cell picture of the card by the linearized (z, t) theory around the CW spatial trajectory:
- the sideband growth rate lambda(Omega) and the band that dominates noise growth, hence n_cell. The conjecture Omega_lambda ~ lambda/|Delta beta_1| is contradicted by a first look (run 2026-10-16-claude-interactive.7-grin-lyapunov, cw_sidebands): relative walk-off raises the sideband growth above lambda(0) (intermodal MI). Derive the MI band from the linearization about the thermalized multimode CW state, and its gain against lambda(0);
- the prediction that a quiet CW beam broadens spectrally only after z_E, to a width of about Omega_lambda;
- the dependence of stochastic-GNLSE (TWA) results on the number of frequency bins and on the window, against the vacuum-energy criterion E' >> E'_vac (exploration note, Section 5).
