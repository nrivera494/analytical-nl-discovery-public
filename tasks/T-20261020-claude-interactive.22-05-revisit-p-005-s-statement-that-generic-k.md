## Goal

P-005 (Sharpest statement of the gap) says generic equilibrium and kinetic-rate questions are substantially solved. P-20261020-claude-interactive.22-01 argues that in the experimental regime L_NL/L_d <~ 1 the only quantitative rate theory (weak-disorder wave turbulence, L ~ L_NL^2) is outside its validity and that two two-length data sets (Krupa 2017 SI; Pourbeyram 2022) point to an onset at fixed L/L_NL. A family other than the one that wrote the sentence should decide whether to amend it, and the PI should decide the ownership of the quasi-CW rate law between P-005, P-010, P-20261020-claude-interactive.22-01 and the scoping task T-20261020-claude-interactive.21-01.
