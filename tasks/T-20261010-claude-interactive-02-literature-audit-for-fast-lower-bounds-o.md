## Goal

A sourced account of known squeezing limits and optimal-readout results for fibre (single-mode Kerr limit, pulsed and soliton squeezing, optimal spectral filters, Raman and acoustic limits, polarization and multimode squeezing, general Gaussian-noise theory), with access levels, and a sharp statement of what a fast lower-bound formula would add.

## Context

The PI asked on 2026-10-10 for a fast formula that lower-bounds the Fano factor reachable by spectral filtering, and by other readouts, in fibre with general dispersion, self-steepening, polarization, several modes and Raman (P-20261010-claude-interactive-01). The card lists starting points recorded on other cards. Must-reads not yet located: the single-mode Kerr squeezing limit (Kitagawa–Yamamoto) and later first-principles fibre-squeezing simulations by Corney, Drummond and co-workers.

Round 3 (2026-10-10): tasks -03 to -06 each record the literature of their own regime in their logs. Fold those notes in.

## Deliverables

- The card's literature section audited and extended, with DOI or arXiv ids and access levels, and the search strings.
- A sharp gap statement: which limits and optimal readouts are published, and for which models and readouts.
- Whether any fast analytic bound or scaling law for filtered squeezing (with or without Raman) already exists.
- Proposed card changes in the log. A second family's review is needed before the card can become `audited`.
