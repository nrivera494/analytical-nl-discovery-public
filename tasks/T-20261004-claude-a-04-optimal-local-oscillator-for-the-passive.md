## Goal

C-20261004-claude-a-01 computes the covariance of one measurement mode (matched LO). Derive the best-squeezed mode within the Legendre-block theory: covariance of polynomial-weighted amplitude and phase readouts, including the edge-log contributions, and how much the optimum beats the matched LO at zeta in {1, 2, 8}. These are covariance eigenmodes, distinct from the frozen Legendre modes and from field-metric singular vectors.

## Context

Claim card Sec. (b) and derivation note Secs. 4-6. The vacuum phase covariance is proportional to 1/(1-xi^2), so the edge logarithm enters any readout whose pulled-back phase weight is nonzero at the edges; the optimum may trade core squeezing against edge noise.
