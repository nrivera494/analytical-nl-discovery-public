## Goal

Repeat the length map of run `2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-length-map` with the anisotropic Raman response (archive `AnisotropicRaman`). Use the same ten lengths (0.25–5 m), the same grid and the same procedure, including the dense pass up to the plateau.

Report the change in $P_{\min}$, $F_{\min}$, $P_{G0}$ and in the local exponent at every length. This turns the Raman-model spread into an error bar on each number in the "Length scaling" bullet of P-20261004-root-01.

## Context

T-20261004-claude-interactive-03 (claude-b.4) ran the oscillator response everywhere. It ran the anisotropic response only at 1 m and 5 m, near the dips (same run, `anisotropic_*` points).

The Raman term is small at the first dip: 0.007–0.03 of $F$, and less than 15% of the slope that ends it. It matters at the second dip found at 5 m, where it is 0.088 of $F$ and supplies 56% of the rising slope.

The run's `run_map.jl` takes `PMIN_KIND=anisotropic`, and `PMIN_DENSE=1` for the dense pass.
