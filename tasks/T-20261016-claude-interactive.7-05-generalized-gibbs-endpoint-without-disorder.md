## Goal

A derivation and test of the long-z state when the dynamics conserves more than N and E_lin:
- the resonant Kerr energy H_res, in the weakly nonlinear regime without disorder;
- in ideal parabolic GRIN, also the centre-of-mass (Kohn) mode, the breathing mode and L_z.

Give the maximum-entropy state under these constraints, and its dependence on the input phases. A coherent input with structured phases and a dephased input of the same mode powers have different H_res. This is the part of the (a)/(b) difference that survives thermalization.

## Context

Run 2026-10-16-claude-interactive.7-rj-toy-thermalization:
- without disorder the toy does not reach RJ (condensation below RJ);
- with weak disorder the dephased launch reaches RJ, but a phase-aligned ("focused") coherent launch relaxes more slowly.

Literature: Podivilov et al. PRL 122, 103902 (2019) (resonant model, 2D-turbulence analogy); Rumpf & Newell (coherent structures plus entropy); arXiv:2512.17777 (anomalous correlators).
