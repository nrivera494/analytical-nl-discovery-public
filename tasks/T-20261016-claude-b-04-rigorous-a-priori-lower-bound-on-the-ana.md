## Goal

A provable upper bound on the DW band yield of the TOD-perturbed (G)NLSE, through an a-priori lower bound on the width $\sigma(\xi)$ of the analyticity strip of solutions. Gevrey-class or radius-of-analyticity persistence estimates of the Kato–Masuda or Bona–Grujić type would give $|\hat u(\xi,w)|\le Ce^{-\sigma(\xi)|w|}$, hence $\eta_B\le C'e^{-2\sigma_{\min}w_z}$.

## Context

Claim C-20261016-claude-b-03 shows that conservation laws alone give no ceiling. Claim C-20261016-claude-b-01 shows the yield is controlled by the strip width $d_*$. A rigorous ceiling must bound $d$ from below in terms of launch data such as energy, Hamiltonian, or the soliton content of the Zakharov–Shabat spectrum.

## Deliverables

- A literature check: radius of spatial analyticity for NLS-type equations.
- A bound, or a counterexample showing that no bound exists in terms of the stated data.
- A comparison with the measured $d_*$ values.
