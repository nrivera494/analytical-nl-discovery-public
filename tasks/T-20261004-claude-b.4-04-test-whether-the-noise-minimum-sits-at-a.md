## Goal

Test whether the location rule seen in the length map survives a change of filter edge and input chirp. The rule has two parts:
- the first noise dip sits where the in-band photon fraction $T_N$ falls to a fixed value;
- equivalently, it sits at a fixed fraction of the classical plateau power.

The rule would be a classical design rule for the quantum minimum: measure output power against input power behind the filter, and read $P_{\min}$ off it. Done means the rule is confirmed or refuted at no fewer than two red-edge offsets $\Omega_eT_0$ and two input chirps $C$, each at no fewer than three lengths.

## Context

claude-b.4 observed the rule in run `2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-length-map` (T-20261004-claude-interactive-03; numbers in its `summary.json` and in `explorations/2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-length.md`). The configuration is 1250 nm LP01 in GIF625, band 1226–1269 nm, input GDD −0.015 ps², oscillator Raman.

**What was seen at 0.4–2 m.**
- The first dip sits at an in-band photon fraction $T_N$ of about 0.97, at an output rms bandwidth of about 11 rad/ps (about half the red-edge offset).
- $P_{\min}/P_{G0}$ stays near 0.37, where $P_{G0}$ is the plateau power ($G=0$).
- $P_{\min}/P_{G025}$ stays near 0.40, where $P_{G025}$ is the power at which $G$ falls below 0.25.
- Over the same range $P_{\min}$ itself changes by a factor 3.4. The exact values and their spread are in the run's summary.

**Where it breaks.**
- The rule fails at 3 and 5 m, where the dip moves to lower $T_N$ and a second dip appears at 5 m.
- It also depends on the chirp. With the GDD set to zero (run `2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-gdd0-check`), $T_N$ at the dip is 0.94 at 0.25 m and 0.85 at 1 m. Any rule has to include $C$.

This is an observation over one filter and one chirp, not a derived law. The hypothesis is claude-b.4's, so any claim built on it needs a non-Claude verifier.

## Deliverables

- Sweeps (oscillator Raman) at two further red-edge positions and at input GDD 0 and +0.015 ps², each at three lengths in 0.5–2 m.
- $T_N$, the output bandwidth and $P_{\min}/P_{G0}$ at each dip.
- A statement of whether the rule holds, and to what tolerance.
- If it holds, a derivation task for the value of $T_N$ at the dip.
