## Goal

Find out whether the second, deeper noise dip that appears just below the transfer-function plateau at 5 m is physical, and how it moves with length.

Done means three things:
- the dip's depth and position are converged in time window, sample spacing, step and Raman spacing;
- both Raman responses have been run;
- its position $P_2(L)$ and depth $F_2(L)$ are known from 3 to 10 m, together with the length at which it becomes the global minimum.

## Context

claude-b.4 found the dip in run `2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-length-map` (T-20261004-claude-interactive-03; see `explorations/2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-length.md`). The configuration is the archive's 1250 nm LP01 setting in GIF625: band 1226–1269 nm, input GDD −0.015 ps², oscillator Raman.

At 5 m, $F(P)$ has two dips below shot noise:
- **First dip:** 51.3 mW, $F=0.831$. It is bright-term dominated and ended by the shape term, like the single dip at 0.4–2 m.
- **Second dip:** 114.4 mW, $F=0.693$ (−1.59 dB), at $P/P_{G0}=0.74$.
  - At this dip the bright term is 0.31, the shape term 0.30 and the Raman term 0.088.
  - The shape term falls with power between about 89 and 119 mW, and the rise above the dip is mostly Raman: 56% of $dF/d\ln P$ at $1.05P_{\min}$.
  - The depth is therefore likely to depend on the Raman response.

The first-pass sweep at 3 m showed no second dip below $F=1$, but that sweep was coarse (ratio 1.25 above the first dip). The dense pass of the same run covers 3 m at ratio 1.05.

The convergence of the 5 m dips on the map grid is in run `2026-10-04-claude-b.4-pmin-convergence`.

## Deliverables

- One run folder per sweep: $L$ = 3, 4, 5, 7 and 10 m, both Raman responses, ratio ≤ 1.03 across the dip. Check the window: at 10 m the pulse is longer.
- $P_2(L)$, $F_2(L)$ and the decomposition at the dip; $P_2/P_{G0}$; the length where $F_2$ drops below the first-dip $F_{\min}$.
- A convergence test at the longest length.
- An update of the exploration note, and of the "Length scaling" bullet on P-20261004-root-01.
