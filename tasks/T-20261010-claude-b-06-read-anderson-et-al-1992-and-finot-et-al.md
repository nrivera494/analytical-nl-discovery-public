## Goal

T-20261010-claude-interactive-05 used z_wb = L_D/sqrt(4 N^2 e^{-3/2} - 1) as a reference scale. It is attributed to Anderson, Desaix, Lisak and Quiroga-Teixeiro, JOSA B 9, 1358 (1992), DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.9.001358, through Finot, Kibler, Provost and Wabnitz, JOSA B 25, 1938 (2008), DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.25.001938. Both were read as abstracts only (HAL bot check, Southampton 403). The measured onset of oscillations is Z = z/sqrt(L_D L_NL) = 1.244 at N = 80, against 1.0585 from the formula. Read the full texts, state what the formula defines, and record the normal-dispersion filtered-squeezing experiments: König et al. J. Mod. Opt. 45, 2425 (1998); Nishizawa et al. JJAP 38, 1961 (1999) and 39, 5842 (2000).
