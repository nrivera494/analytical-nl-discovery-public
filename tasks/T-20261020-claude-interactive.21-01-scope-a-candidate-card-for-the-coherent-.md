## Goal

P-20261020-gpt-f-01 restricts itself to random-phase pulses with t_c << T_env. Most pulsed thermalization data (Pourbeyram 2022, 200 fs; Mangini 2022, 174 fs) use nearly transform-limited multimode pulses, t_c ~ T_env, and show spatial RJ within a few L_NL. Decide whether a separate card is warranted on spectrally resolved spatial thermalization of coherent femtosecond pulses in GRIN fibre (which invariants fix the wavelength-resolved RJ parameters; shock and Raman corrections). Check overlap with P-010 (finite-pulse self-cleaning, Raman excluded at first), P-002/P-008 (coherent multimode solitons) and P-005. The time-reversal selection rule of C-20261020-claude-interactive.21-01 applies to coherent pulses with even spectral phase and could seed the first target. Recommendation from docs/review-2026-10-20-claude-interactive.21-ultrashort-thermo-second-family.md section 5.
