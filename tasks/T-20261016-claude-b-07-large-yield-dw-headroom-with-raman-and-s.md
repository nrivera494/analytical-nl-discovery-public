## Goal

The shaping headroom of DW yield in the large-yield regime, with Raman, self-steepening (photon-conserving form) and length optimisation, using a global or multi-start optimiser. Compare with Petty (2024), about 1.4× from a GDD scan.

## Context

In M0 (no Raman, no shock) the achieved headroom is about 1.5× phase-only and passive, and at most 1.06× from GDD alone. Measured at $N=3$–8, $\delta_3=0.02$–0.05 (claim C-20261016-claude-b-02). Raman lowers small-yield TL yields by large factors and may change the headroom.

## Deliverables

- Headroom at three points in the GNLSE with Raman and shock.
- Its persistence over length.
- A comparison with Petty's GDD gain.
