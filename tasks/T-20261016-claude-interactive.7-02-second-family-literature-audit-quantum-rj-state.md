## Context

The first-family search (log/2026-10-16_claude-interactive.7_T-20261016-claude-interactive.7-01.md) found no paper giving the reduced or joint quantum state, beyond mean occupations, of Kerr-thermalized multimode light. Items to settle:
- Birk, Lyubarov, Ruimy, Even-Tzur, Wu, Cohen, Segev, Kaminer & Sidorenko, CLEO 2023 FTh4B.2 (DOI 10.1364/CLEO_FS.2023.FTh4B.2): find any full paper or thesis chapter, and say what it derives.
- Vardi, Ramos & Kottos, arXiv:2409.01422: does it already give the finite-M microcanonical means of the polytope ensemble (exploration note, Section 4)? Does it give fluctuations?
- Condensate-fluctuation and phase-spreading literature for closed (microcanonical) Bose systems, e.g. Sinatra, Castin & Witkowska, and Witkowska, Gajda & Rzazewski on classical fields with a cutoff: is the Dirichlet law, g2 = 2 - 2/M, or the condensate-variance formula already published for a classical field with a finite number of modes?
- Wave-turbulence one-mode PDF theory (Choi, Lvov & Nazarenko; Nazarenko's book): confirm the Fokker-Planck form used for the dephased route.
- Disorder-assisted condensation in fibre (Fusaro et al.; Sidelnikov et al.) and any statement of the resonant invariant H_res in GRIN thermalization (Podivilov et al. 2019).
