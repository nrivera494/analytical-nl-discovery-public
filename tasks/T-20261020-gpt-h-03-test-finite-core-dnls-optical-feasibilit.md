## Goal

Establish a calibrated reduced observation window for a specified coupled-core fibre/graph. Classify each launch using phase-dependent full energy, separate ring and hexagonal spectra, and test finite-size localization rather than importing a thermodynamic line as an exact small-N threshold. Compute pump/Stokes gain with evolving localized power, group-velocity walkoff, coupling dispersion, pulse reshaping and loss. Demonstrate an early formation/exchange statistic accessible before perturbations, or report that the proposed realization fails. Do not assume a long coarsening time is observable merely because pulses are short.
