## Goal

An estimate of F, by a second numerical method, for at least one of the registered LP01 baselines with Raman on. The method is nonlinear stochastic (truncated-Wigner) propagation with vacuum input noise and the Raman Langevin drive, instead of the linearized adjoint. The estimate comes with its statistical error and is compared with the registered value.

Done means one of the following:
- the stochastic F agrees with the registered value within its statistical error;
- the difference is quantified and traced to a convention, for example Wigner ordering of the counted photon number or the normalization of the Raman noise spectrum.

## Context

- The three baselines registered under T-20261004-claude-interactive-06 reproduce the archive bit for bit, but with the archive's own code. Only case 1 (Kerr only) has an independent check, the analytical Green function.
  - Case 2: `runs/2026-10-04-claude-b.1-prepared-sech-readout-20m`, F = 0.0323 / 0.0529.
  - Case 3: `runs/2026-10-04-claude-b.1-bandpass-1546-1553-20m`, F = 0.370 / 0.415.
  - For cases 2 and 3, a second method is missing (AGENTS.md, Evidence standards: "When two solvers are available, use both").
- The solver has a stochastic route: `stochastic_ensemble`, `stochastic_solve`, `RamanLangevin`, `stochastic_values` and `fano_factor` in `solver/src/stochastic.jl`, through the `PulsePropagationProblem` interface. The archive notes that the package's optional Raman noise spectrum uses a different, asymmetric convention from the symmetric one used in the adjoint contraction (`research_runs/2026-09-23_squeezing/raman_noise.jl`). Check that before comparing.
- Case 3 is the cheapest to resolve. At F ≈ 0.37, about 400 trajectories give a 7 % standard error on the variance. Case 2 (F ≈ 0.03 against a reference) needs far more samples or a variance-reduced estimator.
