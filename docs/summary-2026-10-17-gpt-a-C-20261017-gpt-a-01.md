# Stronger phase-only witnesses, with unresolved optima

The frozen masks numerically support

$$H_\phi=\sup_\phi\frac{f_1(\phi)}{f_1(0)}\ge
\begin{cases}1.021&(N,T_0)=(3,140\,{\rm fs}),\\
1.104&(4,200\,{\rm fs}),\\1.180&(5,280\,{\rm fs}).\end{cases}$$

Here $f_1=2\operatorname{Im}\zeta_1/N^2$ is the isolated leading soliton's ZS
energy fraction, $\zeta_1$ its eigenvalue for $q=Nu$, $u(0)=\operatorname{sech}(t/T_0)$,
$N$ soliton order, and $\phi$ spectral phase. The three points lie within
$3\le N\le5$, $0.784\le N^2\tau_1/T_0\le1.090$, with
$NL/L_D=4$, $L_D=T_0^2/|\beta_2|$, $f_R=0.18$,
$\tau_2/\tau_1=32/12.2$, and zero higher dispersion, shock and loss.
No continuous N law is asserted.

A 30-coefficient discrete-adjoint search used three initial starts per N.
Independent gated ZS readout gives $H=1.02211\pm0.00012$,
$1.10594\pm0.00004$, and $1.18551\pm0.00038$; errors are numerical
discrepancy estimates. Parseval proves fixed input energy; the bounds are
numerical witnesses. At matched local TL duration peaks, gains remain
1.62%, 10.46% and 18.01%. Frozen duration, material and N=6-transfer tests pass.
Step-error orders are 3.86–3.96; extraction order is approximately 2.

Only N=3 passes the two-grid $\|\nabla_c f_1\|_2<10^{-5}$ target.
N=4 and N=5 remain unresolved optima. N=5's fraction is stable over three
windows, but its full waveform remains window-sensitive. No global upper
bound or broadband fibre certificate follows.
