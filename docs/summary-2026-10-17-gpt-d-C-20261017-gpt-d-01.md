# Exact sech tightness and corrected asymptotes

For $I=E\,\mathrm{sech}^2(t/T)/(2T)$ and the normalized Raman oscillator
$h_R(t)=(a^2+b^2)e^{-at}\sin(bt)/b$,

$$\boxed{\rho(T)=\operatorname{Re}\!\left[
(1+ia/b)\{1-s+\tfrac12s^2\psi_1(1+s/2)\}\right],\qquad s=(a-ib)T.}$$

$a=1/\tau_2$ is damping rate, $b=1/\tau_1$ resonance angular frequency,
$\psi_1$ trigamma, and $\rho=|E'|/(\kappa_UE^2)$, where
$E=\int I\,dt$, $E'=dE/dz$, and
$\kappa_U=n_2f_R(a^2+b^2)/(2cA_{\rm eff})$.
$n_2,f_R,c,A_{\rm eff}$ are nonlinear index, Raman fraction, light speed
and mode area. With $x=T/\tau_1$ and $r=\tau_1/\tau_2$,

$$\rho=\frac{16r}{15(1+r^2)^2}x^{-3}[1+O_r(x^{-2})],
\qquad 1-\rho=2rx+O_r(x^2).$$

**Regime and proof.** The exact identity holds for $0<x,r<\infty$ in
the scalar lossless constant-area photon-conserving model. The two limits
are $x\to\infty$ and $x\max(1,r)\to0$ at fixed $r$.
Invert the Raman spectral weight and integrate against the sech
intensity autocorrelation; its Laplace transform gives trigamma.
Taylor remainders give the stated orders. More generally,
$\rho=8\mu_1/(15kT^3)-16\mu_3/(63kT^5)+O(T^{-7})$,
where $k=h_R'(0^+)$ and $\mu_j=\int_0^\infty t^jh_R(t)dt$;
finite absolute fifth moment controls the remainder.
