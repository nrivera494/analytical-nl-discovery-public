# Measured-response ZBLAN test of peak-law transfer

This is a proposer test report. Independent Claude-family verification is
T-20261017-gpt-c-05. Neither these tests nor their novelty search can mark
this GPT-proposed card independently verified.

## Why the material test did not assume width selection

Phase 1 rejected the exit-width hypothesis. At the N=16 share peaks, fitted
sech widths were 25.427, 28.504 and 27.317 fs for BW, HC and the As-like
oscillator, against response-only maxima 15.558, 17.709 and 19.147 fs:
approximately +63%, +61% and +43%. Full fitted-width intervals and the
independent area-width intervals all exclude the frozen ±15% equality.
The converged BW N=4 near-optimum T0=200 fs case separately gives 21.323 fs
fitted and 19.405 fs area width against 15.558 fs. These numbers belong to
run `2026-10-17-gpt-c-raman-sharpening`, `final_metrics.json` and `accuracy.json`.
They reject an **exit-width** rule, not the published overlap maximum or a
possible transient compression/overlap mechanism.

## Source-first freeze and model

Before any ZBLAN GNLSE propagation, source-only quadrature gave an overlap
maximum 22.0557541 fs (doubled quadrature 22.0557548 fs) and inverse angular
gain-peak frequency tau_g=9.23147930 fs. The measured response is Yan's
eight-mode fit, DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.29.000238 (abstract plus public table and
first-page preview; full article inaccessible). The broadening convention
was checked against HC, DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.19.002886 (full text, Table 1
footnote), and Kormokar 2023 Eq. (27), DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.478975 /
arXiv:2301.02945 (full text selected). It is not a single oscillator.

The published fibre model is Kormokar 2021, DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.409240,
Sec. 4.2.1 (full text selected): pump 1940 nm, beta2=-18.9 ps²/km,
beta3=.112 ps³/km, gamma=2.1 W^-1 km^-1 and loss 50 dB/km. Physical runs include
shock, cubic dispersion and loss; reference runs remove those three terms.
All use scalar deterministic transform-limited sech inputs, fR=.24 and
LN/LD=4. This combines a bulk measurement with a separate published fibre
model, not a same-spool calibration. Agger's measured fibre, DOI
10.1364/JOSAB.29.000635 (full text, selected Sec.4), has a substantially
different Raman fraction. Numerical error bars do not cover that variation.

Commit `b042d48fd03e65791b577d869b585e36fd48b07f` froze all four material
orders before propagation. The gain-peak rescaling was deliberately risky:
$f_1^*\sqrt N=1.29\pm10\%$,
$T_0^*=1.70\tau_gN^{1.62}\pm30\%$ and the resulting input-area energy
prediction ±45%. The already rejected $T_1=T_R^*\pm15\%$ was frozen as a
separate null. Every new material point is held out from the silica fits;
no material result is used to refit a prediction.

Our overlap normalization is
$R(T)=(\pi/2)\int_0^\infty x^3[-\operatorname{Im}\widehat h_R(x/T)]
\operatorname{csch}^2(\pi x/2)\,dx$, with unit-integral causal hR and
Fourier convention exp(-i*Omega*t). The constant fR is outside R; this
does not affect its maximum. The isolated-sech frequency-moment calculation
gives a shift rate proportional to $R(T)/T$, at fixed peak power. It does
not derive a fission selection rule. Durations here are sech parameters,
not intensity FWHM.

## Measurement and qualification

Independent Float64 RK4IP, exact causal Raman transform, sparse checkpoints
and local Zakharov–Shabat extraction are documented in the run scripts.
The leading component must be both reddest and largest, have a small gate
boundary and agree with directly gated energy within the frozen extraction
tolerance. Report fitted-sech and area-inferred widths separately. Photon
fractions integrate spectral energy divided by absolute photon frequency;
they are not inferred from the energy share.

Every optimum needs qualified neighbors on both sides. Geometric midpoint
refinements localize the maximum without modifying its predicted value.
The table retains full neighboring-duration brackets, coordinate-fit
sensitivity and the declared numerical allowances. A pass requires the
entire measurement interval inside the frozen tolerance; a failure requires
it entirely outside. Anything straddling a boundary remains unresolved.

The narrow reference N=6 window failed. Three affected endpoints are
excluded, including one caught by the final-checkpoint edge diagnostic.
The new analyzer initially omitted that instantaneous diagnostic while
checking the periodically accumulated maximum; both are now required.
Phase 1 already checked both and its accepted peak endpoints were rechecked.
Window recovery and a fixed-dt doubled-window control were frozen in
`df140a3`; midpoint localization began from `5cfc27b`. The one 65536-point

## Novelty and limits

The four broad parts are partly published; the exact fission selection and
simple order-wide laws were not found in the bounded search, with medium
confidence in that negative finding. The material response and ZBLAN
enhancement themselves are published. Closest sources, access levels and
the extended chalcogenide/tellurite/fluoride/thesis queries are in the
[novelty review](../../docs/review-2026-10-17-gpt-c-raman-peak-novelty.md).

No physical asymptotic remainder, infinite-order limit, global duration
unimodality or output-length-independent limiting partition is established.
The model keeps mode area and loss constant and truncates dispersion at
third order. Local-ZS energy is an operational soliton measure; gated
energy, photon fractions and spectral support remain separate. A conditional
simulation counterexample is not a same-spool experimental determination.
