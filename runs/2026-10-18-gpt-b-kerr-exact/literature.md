# Independent literature check (2026-10-18)
Agent gpt-b; model gpt-6-astra; family gpt. This search used no proposer scripts, notes, logs or PR descriptions.

- Bajer, Miranowicz and Tanaś, *Limits of noise squeezing in Kerr effect*, DOI **10.1023/A:1021867510898**, **arXiv:quant-ph/0212158**, **full text** (four-page preprint). Equations (4), (6), (7) give exact coherent Kerr variance, its tangent approximation, and the leading curvature correction. Their optimization holds interaction time fixed while varying power, hence their printed 2/5 exponent is not a contradiction of the fixed-photon-number n^(-1/3) optimum. Substituting tau=s/n in their equation (7) yields the coherent balance used here. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0212158).
- Najafabadi, Klimov, Sánchez-Soto and Leuchs, *Quasiclassical approach to the nonlinear Kerr dynamics*, DOI **10.1016/j.optcom.2023.129717**, **full text, sections 2–3 and conclusions** (publisher text plus author-institution PDF). Equation (31) explicitly gives the coherent optimum 3/2 (2r)^(-2/3), with r=sqrt(n), i.e. the 0.94494 n^(-1/3) coefficient. It also compares the classical Wigner flow with exact evolution. The coherent exponent and coefficient are therefore published, not new to the card. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optcom.2023.129717).
- Kitagawa and Yamamoto, DOI **10.1103/PhysRevA.34.3974**, **abstract only**. Crescent-shaped number-preserving Kerr evolution and interferometric photon-number reduction were already discussed. This abstract alone is not evidence for the present multimode robustness statement. [Primary abstract](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.34.3974).
- Sloan et al., **arXiv:2509.03482**, **full text, main article** (supplement not accessed in this audit). Multimode noise propagation by sensitivities, wavefront control and self/cross-phase mechanisms overlap with the broad tangent-noise framework. The main article does not establish the proposed deep-squeezing curvature bound. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.03482v1).
- Tacey and Corney, DOI **10.1364/OL.38.002991**, **arXiv:1305.0633**, **full text**. Their fibre Sagnac polarization-squeezing calculation includes Raman noise, loss, GAWBS and pulse effects; these can limit observable squeezing. Its geometry and observable differ from the ideal monochromatic dark-port model tested here. Their explicit loss example supports testing a modest aggregate loss, but does not calibrate the GRIN fibre in this card. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1305.0633).
- Garnier et al., DOI **10.1103/PhysRevA.100.053835**, **arXiv:2011.03918**, **full text, disorder-model and discussion sections**. Their matrix disorder acts on polarization. Equating a numerical strength with the full spatial-group GUE process in our independent model requires a separate covariance normalization and physical calibration. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.03918).
- Kitagawa and Ueda, DOI **10.1103/PhysRevA.47.5138**, **abstract only** (the primary search also exposed the introductory PDF page). One-axis twisting and interferometric spin squeezing are established prior art. The number-conserving bright-reference Sagnac benchmark belongs to this distinction between spin/counting observables and ordinary single-mode quadratures; no new interferometric exponent is claimed here. [Primary abstract](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.47.5138).
- Chapman and Peters, DOI **10.1364/OPTCON.485636**, **arXiv:2211.01433**, **full text, sections 2 and 3C**. Their covariance treatment of Kerr/Sagnac squeezing includes excess pump noise, added polarization noise and detection loss. It supports treating the phase reference and noise location explicitly; it does not supply a non-Gaussian curvature bound or the present finite-jitter coefficient. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.01433).
- Vintskevich, Grigoriev and Filippov, DOI **10.1103/PhysRevA.100.053811**, **arXiv:1905.05756**, **full text, Kerr-modulation section and appendix introduction**. Their equation (70) explicitly observes that Kerr leaves thermal and phase-averaged inputs unchanged, and their subsequent analysis treats Kerr-modulated displaced thermal pumps for parametric generation. This locates prior art for the no-go premise; that section does not state the optimized mixedness squeezing coefficient tested here. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.05756).
