# Primary-source check

Access levels below distinguish full-text passages read from merely locating a
paper. The search does not establish exhaustive novelty clearance.

- **Fusaro et al. (2019)**, DOI [10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.123902](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.123902), [arXiv:2011.05111](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.05111). **Full text**, main theory and disorder discussion. The scale ordering is linear beat length much shorter than disorder length, itself shorter than nonlinear length. Their disorder acts on two polarization components of each spatial mode; our full spatial-group GUE noise differs. In the kinetic regime the collision rate is proportional to Kerr strength squared divided by disorder strength. Thus stronger disorder can slow exchange within that regime while disorder unlocks exchange relative to the ordered coherent system. Their experimentally relevant many-mode setting does not calibrate the numerical coefficient of a fifteen-mode simulation.
- **Podivilov et al. (2019)**, DOI [10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.103902](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.103902), arXiv:1811.10239. **Full text**, main theory equations 4–5 read from the APS PDF. The resonant GRIN reduction and a phase-dependent three-mode Hamiltonian are already explicit. This is prior art for phase-sensitive dynamics and resonant energy, not a proof of unrestricted microcanonical equilibration.
- **Wu et al. (2022)**, DOI [10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.123901](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.123901), [arXiv:2112.10841](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10841). **Full text**, theory equations 2–7. Optical generalized Gibbs ensembles with power, linear energy and angular momentum, yielding a generalized RJ modal law, are published. This is different from conditioning on the quartic resonant Hamiltonian.
- **Germain, Hani and Thomann, I**, [arXiv:1501.03760](https://arxiv.org/html/1501.03760). **Full text**, sections 1–3 and invariant-subspace results. The harmonic-trap resonant equation, its symmetries, angular momentum and additional untruncated conserved bilinears are established. Normal-form approximation is a finite-length statement; it does not justify an arbitrarily long fibre extrapolation.
- **Germain, Hani and Thomann, II**, [arXiv:1502.05643](https://arxiv.org/html/1502.05643). **Full text**, sections 2 and 3.1–3.2. They construct invariant Gibbs measures; the finite-dimensional approximation has density proportional to exp(-beta H_res) relative to a Gaussian reference involving linear energy. Liouville's theorem proves stationarity. This substantially overlaps the proposed invariant-measure core. It does not prove attraction or mixing of every initial state on a shell.
- **Biasi et al. (2018)**, DOI [10.1103/PhysRevE.98.032222](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.98.032222), [arXiv:1805.01775](https://arxiv.org/html/1805.01775v2). **Full text**, resonant-system and invariant-sector sections. Further charges and recurrent special solutions reinforce the need to specify the accessible sector before invoking ergodicity.
- **Biasi, Evnin and Malomed (2023)**, DOI [10.1103/PhysRevE.108.034204](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.108.034204), [arXiv:2304.10308](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.10308v2). **Full text**, spectral and discussion sections. Equidistant traps can obstruct ergodicity; the multidimensional calculations impose spherical symmetry. This is a caution relevant to GRIN, not a theorem covering all of our finite-model launches.
- **Vardi, Ramos and Kottos (2024)**, DOI [10.1103/PhysRevResearch.6.043282](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.6.043282), [arXiv:2409.01422](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.01422). **Full text**, microcanonical construction and classical comparisons. Finite-mode microcanonical departures from canonical/RJ predictions are published. Their lattice model and central-energy examples differ from the condensed single-ground-mode simplex identity tested here. The PDF text search found no occurrence of “Dirichlet”; that alone is not a novelty determination.
