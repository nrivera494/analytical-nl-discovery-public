# Primary-source comparison

This is an independent literature read during clean context. It does not use other agents' reviews, histories or notes. Bibliographic numerical parameters used in calculation are recorded in `algebra_summary.json` and subsequent material-sensitivity runs.

- **Kormokar, Shamim and Rochette (2021), DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.409240; full text** (author preprint, https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/e7f5/ff2b482f21657c3860c55fb42e0c94d43cae.pdf). Equations 23 and 29–33 supply the energy drift and adiabatic width elimination. Equations 40 and 49 give the constant-loss effective length; the later high-order formula neglects Stokes loss in that integration. I did not find a photon-number fourth-power invariant for a frequency-dependent absorption edge or an integrated-photon cutoff optimum. The constant-loss stall is already implicit in their bounded effective length. Claim 01's general integral is a short elimination of propagation distance from these familiar drift equations, with a photon-conserving coordinate; its algebraic novelty is modest.
- **Kormokar, Shamim and Rochette (2023), DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.478975, arXiv:2301.02945; full text** (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.02945). Equations 12–13 restate the constant-loss drift/effective-length law. The paper's main subject is fission energy transfer and its Raman-dependent correction. No frequency-resolved fourth-power photon budget, exponential-edge terminal condition or cutoff optimum was found in the inspected full text.
- **Petersen et al. (2017), DOI 10.1364/OE.25.015336; full text** (https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/134034570/oe_25_13_15336.pdf). Sections 4–5 compare cutback/taper geometries and their output bandwidth and long-wavelength power. Loss-driven length optimization is established physically and numerically. No closed-form single-soliton photon-cutoff optimum or frequency-space invariant was found. Their multi-soliton, taper and dispersive-wave setting is broader than the proposed scalar isolated-soliton reduction.
- **Boilard, Vallee and Bernier, arXiv:2109.13366; full text** (https://arxiv.org/html/2109.13366). Table 2 gives the cladding Sellmeier coefficients used here. The claim's Z75 geometry is a specified model built with those material coefficients; it is not the measured ZrF4 geometry in Table 1. Selective LP01 excitation is required at the claim's launch because the specified waveguide is not strictly single-mode there.
- **Gauthier et al. (2022), DOI 10.1038/s41598-022-19658-8; full text** (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9508244/). The measured Raman fit is for InF3. Its broad component is not a measured ZrF4 response. The paper explicitly describes uncertainty in fitted nonlinear parameters; it does not establish the claim's ZrF4 nonlinear index and single-oscillator response together.
- **Xia et al. (2006), DOI 10.1364/OL.31.002553; full text** (https://islam.engin.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/57/2017/12/Laser-link-1.pdf). The final page reports intrinsic-edge attenuation measured in a multimode ZBLAN fibre with similar glass composition. I fit an exponential to those reported points as an independent slope/normalization sensitivity, without pretending they characterize Z75. The paper's nonlinear-index value refers onward to a review, rather than reporting a measurement of that coefficient.
- **Agger et al. (2012), DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.29.000635; full text** (https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/7652877/2012_SCG_in_ZBLAN_fibers_detailed_comparison_between_measurement_and_simulation.pdf). Sections 3–4 give the mode-area transformation, a measured two-band Raman gain and discussion of composition/drawing dependence. The nonlinear index used in their propagation is adopted from earlier literature. Their long-wavelength attenuation data are explicitly estimates beyond the measured interval, so I do not relabel that plotted edge a direct measurement.
- **Yan et al. (2012), DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.29.000238; abstract only, plus publisher table and first-page preview** (https://opg.optica.org/josab/abstract.cfm?uri=josab-29-2-238). The abstract reports a bulk ZBLAN third-order susceptibility measurement. Full measurement details and conversion to a fibre n2 were not accessible; Agger's discussion of its larger adopted n2 is secondary evidence, not an independently checked replacement calibration.
- **Adair, Chase and Payne (1987), DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.4.000875; abstract only, plus publisher tables** (https://opg.optica.org/josab/abstract.cfm?uri=josab-4-6-875). This is a primary nonlinear-index measurement including a fluorozirconate sample. Composition/wavelength/unit-convention transfer to the specified Z75 fibre was not established; its value is not substituted as if it were a measurement of that fibre.
- **Parker (1989), DOI 10.1146/annurev.ms.19.080189.000321; not accessed** (publisher bibliographic entry located). This is the review cited by Xia for its nonlinear-index value. It is not itself a new primary measurement.
- **Kormokar and Rochette (2024), DOI 10.1364/JOSAB.521085; abstract only and first-page preview** (https://opg.optica.org/josab/abstract.cfm?uri=josab-41-7-1628). This additional comparison is requested by the verify task. It describes moment equations with gain and wavelength-dependent fibre properties, and an optimum initial chirp. Full article access was unavailable; the author's thesis was located in McGill's catalogue, but its download was inaccessible. Absence of the proposed invariant or optimum in this paper is not established.
- **Rosenberg et al. (2020), DOI 10.1364/OE.383014; full text** (indexed author-uploaded article at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/338196038_Boosting_few-cycle_soliton_self-frequency_shift_using_negative_prechirp; publisher HTML located but not rendered). The experiment uses few-cycle, higher-order inputs and a localized OH absorption band. Negative prechirp changes the power partition at fission. The paper explicitly distinguishes that case from fundamental solitons, for which nearly zero chirp works best. The longer-pulse, lossless shaping search therefore does not contradict this experiment and cannot justify a general statement that GDD never helps. Numerical regime parameters are recorded in the auditor's literature run.
