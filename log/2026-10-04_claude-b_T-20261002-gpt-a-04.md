## Found

- **P-007 (Mamyshev oscillator map).**
  - The fixed points and their bifurcation exist numerically in the passive iterated regenerator map: eigenpulses, multiple eigenpulses and limit cycles (Pitois et al. 2007, 2008).
  - Floquet analysis is used for self-starting (Bednyakova et al. 2021).
  - Pulsations, period doubling and chaos have been observed and simulated (Cao 2022; Yan 2023; Wang 2024).
  - No few-coordinate map with derived multipliers was found.
  - Closest unread item: an ICLO 2022 abstract titled "Bifurcations and multiple-period soliton pulsations in Mamyshev oscillator".
- **P-010 (pulsed self-cleaning).**
  - The theory is quasi-CW spatial (Podivilov 2019; Garnier 2019; Baudin 2023) or stationary spatiotemporal (Zanaglia).
  - Baudin et al. 2023 state that a quantitative description would require analysis of the temporal averaging inherent to pulsed measurements; I read the passage.
  - Zitelli 2024 limits the thermodynamic description to $L\lesssim$ a few $L_D$ with an error metric; I read the passage. It has no walk-off term.
  - The card's target, a derived criterion in $T_0$, $L_D$, modal delay spread and $L_{NL}$, is open.
- **P-008 (soliton self-mode conversion).**
  - Rishøj 2019 has no transfer or onset model.
  - All SSMC follow-ups found are experiments or numerics.
  - Neighbouring reductions: moment equations for a different Raman modal transfer (Liu 2025), the polarization analogue (Stefańska 2022), and incoherent-wave kinetics (Baudin 2022).
  - The natural onset template is quantum-initiated Stokes statistics (Raymer–Mostowski 1981; Walmsley–Raymer 1983), not yet applied to fiber modes.
- **P-011 (quartic-soliton noise).**
  - No squeezing, covariance or Gordon–Haus theory for PQS was found.
  - The sharpest ingredient: PQS lack Galilean invariance (Widjaja 2021). The frequency-to-velocity coefficient that sets Gordon–Haus jitter must come from the moving PQS family.
  - Method templates exist: Li–Spiller–Biondini 2007; Grigoryan–Menyuk–Mu 1999; Shamailov–Erkintalo 2026; Gustin et al. 2026.
  - Zou 2025 (the only PQS noise paper) was not accessible to this review.
