## Found

**Model and convention.** $\partial_zA=-\tfrac i2\beta_2A_{TT}+i\gamma|A|^2A+\tfrac g2A+F$ with $\langle F^\dagger F\rangle=gn_{sp}\hbar\omega_0\delta\delta$ and $[F,F^\dagger]=-g\hbar\omega_0\delta\delta$. All predictions are symmetric (Wigner) covariances, with strength $g(n_{sp}-\tfrac12)\hbar\omega_0$.

**Orchestrator sketch: confirmed** (Claude-family re-derivation; gpt-a's check is the cross-family one).
- The parabolic family with gain, the width ODE, and the rescaled equation $s''+\tfrac23s'+\tfrac s9=\tfrac1{9s^2}$ with Lyapunov function $V$ (G1, G2). Six launches converge numerically (C3). This is global convergence within the dispersionless parabolic family only.
- The Legendre separation about any parabolic background, with gain (G3).
- The block equation and the asymptotic rates $\tfrac g3[-1\pm\sqrt{1-l(l+1)/2}]$ (G4).
- The phase-sensitive statement: on the asymptote, the shape modes' photon-normalized quadratures have power-gain exponents 1.674 and 0.326 at $gL=24$, converging to 5/3 and 1/3 against 1 for a linear amplifier (C8).

**Exact collective-coordinate channel.** For flat gain, any pulse and any $N$:
- the energy noise is exactly the linear amplifier's;
- $(T_c,\Omega_c)$ is driven by ASE with covariance $\tfrac{2D}{E}W(z)$, where $W$ is the pulse's time–frequency covariance, plus the input $W(0)/N_0$;
- compressed timing is $T_c+D_c\Omega_c$.

So the attraction acts on these coordinates only by shaping $W(z)$. On the similariton asymptote, in closed form:
- centroid-frequency noise figure: $1+3(2n_{sp}-1)(1-G^{-1/3})$, against $1+(2n_{sp}-1)(1-G^{-1})$ for a linear amplifier;
- compressed-pulse timing variance: grows as $(gL)^2$ instead of saturating;
- mechanism: an ASE kick moves time and frequency along the local chirp, and is magnified by $\tfrac g3(L-z')$ after compression.

**Shape modes.**
- The intensity-shape quadratures are de-amplified relative to the energy mode by $G^{-2/3}$.
- The single-shot spectral covariance (low-order Legendre modes) tends to rank one. Its $\lambda_2/\lambda_1$ falls with slope −0.652 per $gL$, against the predicted −2/3.
- The matched-LO phase noise becomes energy-locked: $1-r^2$ falls with slope −0.648 per $gL$.
- The uniform (Kerr) phase noise is $\tfrac49[\theta_L^2+(2n_{sp}-1)\Gamma\int e^{-\Gamma\zeta}(\theta_L-\theta)^2]/(\varepsilon_0^2N_0)$, checked in closed form (C11).

**Vacuum metric and edges.** The intensity part of the noise couples $l\leftrightarrow l\pm2$ through $\int(1-\xi^2)P_lP_m$; this is kept exactly. The phase part is edge-singular. It enters every readout considered at relative order $\varepsilon_{\rm eff}^2\ln(1/\varepsilon_{\rm eff})$, with $\varepsilon_{\rm eff}\propto e^{-2gz/3}$ and edge cut-off $\propto e^{-4gz/9}$.

**Realistic seed** (Gaussian, 250 fs, 10 pJ; $gL=6.08$; $\varepsilon_0=1.8$, so not semiclassical). With $W(z)$ from noise-free runs, the exact formula predicts a compressed-pulse timing variance 3.55 times the linear amplifier's at $n_{sp}=1$: rms 39 as against 21 as. The centroid-frequency variance is only 9% larger.

**Solver gain noise (claude-a.1; oracle check).**
- `ShotNoise(0.5)` with `GainLangevin(ns)` is a Wigner amplifier with $n_{sp}=2ns^2+\tfrac12$.
- Photon-number Fano-factor ratio 0.9997 ± 0.0018 over 12 cases; G = e, e², e³; n_sp = 1, 2; dz halved; N0 = 1e6, 1e8; each source switched off in turn.
- The noise is flat across frequency.
- Kerr was off. With Kerr on, the operator-splitting placement of the kick is untested.
