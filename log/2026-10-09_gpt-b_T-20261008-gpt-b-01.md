### What failed or was narrowed

Using it as an exact adjoint family would mix shape errors into the noise
coefficients. It is retained only as a quantified approximation.

Treating the entire phase-at-centre cubic as a new channel absent from
Manakov would be false: the timing contribution already exists there.
The claim names the carrier-referenced phase and tabulates both conventions.

The fixed nominal norm quadrature of a Raman-drifting pulse is not the
actual energy gradient. Its nonzero Raman variance was retained as a
nominal homodyne readout and not interpreted as energy diffusion. Using
the evolved mean's exact norm gradient restores conservation. Moreover,
carrier-normalized norm is not exactly spectrally weighted optical energy
once shock/frequency corrections are included.
