## Found

- **-01 (gas pulse-train instability).**
  - **Not found:** no pulse-to-pulse map or slow ODE for the onset and period of the pulsations.
  - **What exists:**
    - steady buildup analyses;
    - diffusion-time scaling (Schade 2021);
    - mitigation by mixtures and purging (Koehler CLEO/Europe 2021; Tani 2022);
    - a non-periodic gas-flow onset criterion at Reynolds number about 2040 (Gebhardt 2021);
    - feedback analogues in other geometries: cavity–plasma bistability (Allison 2011) and filament density-hole beam steering (Jhajj 2013).
- **-02 (multimode SBS exceedance).**
  - Stochastic SBS is simulated with depletion, transverse modes, two-mode fiber and polarization (Gaeta–Boyd 1991; Fotiadi 2002; Afshaarvahid 2001; Armstrong 2015; Bowers 2020).
  - Analytic results exist only for the undepleted or linear case (Boyd 1990; Nieves 2021). The closest analytic tail theory is Mounaix's instanton analysis of a linear amplifier driven by the square of a Gaussian (speckle) field, which has no depletion.
  - A depletion-corrected finite-window exceedance law remains open.
- **-03 (modal phase coherence).**
  - An explicit disorder-averaged coherence law exists, but only in the disorder-dominated regime (Garnier et al. 2019; I read the passage). Correlations are slaved as $\gamma G_{pq}/(4\Delta\beta-i\Delta\beta_{pq})$ and vanish beyond $L_{nl}$ when $L_d\ll L_{nl}$.
  - Anomalous-correlator kinetics (Guasoni 2017; Torres 2026) and attractor theory (Pitois 2005; Guasoni–Kozlov–Wabnitz 2012; Turitsyn–Wabnitz 2013; Ji 2023) cover two-component or counterpropagating systems.
  - The open part narrows to the weak-disorder locking regime of co-propagating self-cleaning, and its crossover to the known decoherence limit.
  - The helper quoted a rate of $3\Delta\beta$ from the Fusaro PRL supplement; I did not read that supplement. The cards quote only the $4\Delta\beta$ form that I read in Garnier et al.
